<Note>
  This section documents content conventions for the **Mzizi registry** and the applications
  built on it — not **Mzizi-lang**. This site's own editorial rule for the language is
  different and stricter; see [status](/status).
</Note>

Content in the bundu ecosystem is **clear, direct and respectful**. It is written for a diverse
African audience — people who may speak English as a second or third language, who may be
reading on a low-bandwidth connection, and who deserve the same quality of communication as
readers anywhere.

family of products feel like one. *Ndiri nekuti tiri* — I am because we are.
