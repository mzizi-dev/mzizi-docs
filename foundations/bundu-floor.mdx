The Bundu floor is a set of hard minimums in **two tiers, by pointer**. Touch (a coarse
pointer, and every device that is not a desktop) keeps the high numbers: a 49px hit region and
17px body text. A desktop (a fine pointer with hover, 64rem and up) keeps a 28px hit region and
15px body text. Captions, the spacing floor and contrast are the same on both. Bundu sets the
standard for outdoor reading and for people who find technology hard (older users, African and
emerging markets), so the touch minimums are as high as they can be and one Bundu will stand
by. It shipped in registry 4.23.0 (3 November 2026) and `@bundu/ui` 0.8.0 (owner rulings of 1
and 2 November 2026; tracking issue
[mzizi-registry#597](https://github.com/mzizi-dev/mzizi-registry/issues/597)). The desktop tier
came with registry 4.36.0 (12 November 2026), on the owner's ruling "yes do the device-based
floor, desktop body 15px"
([mzizi-registry#660](https://github.com/mzizi-dev/mzizi-registry/issues/660)): a 49px hit
region and 17px body on every desktop screen cost a great deal of scrolling, and Apple's own
macOS defaults are a 28 pt control and 13 pt body.

## What the floor is

| Floor                              | Touch       | Desktop     | Derives from                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body text                          | 17px        | 15px        | Apple's iOS default body size (a prime); the owner's desktop size |
| Captions, metadata, secondary text | 14px        | 14px        | 2 × 7, the radius unit                                            |
| Hit region of every control        | 49px        | 28px        | 7 × 7, above Apple's 44 pt; 4 × 7, Apple's macOS default control  |
| Smallest a control is drawn        | 28px        | 28px        | 4 × 7                                                             |
| Contrast                           | 7 / 4.5 / 3 | 7 / 4.5 / 3 | WCAG 2: body text, non-body text, UI parts                        |

The floor also sets minimums for space: 14px inset, 7px gaps and 1.5 body line height, on
[the spacing floor](/foundations/spacing-floor) page.

Nothing readable is smaller than 14px, and nothing interactive has a hit region smaller than
its tier's minimum: 49px on touch, 28px on a desktop (above the 24px of WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8). The
numbers live in one place in the registry, `BUNDU_FLOOR` in `lib/tokens/roles.source.ts`, with
the desktop tier under `pointerFine`; the token generators, the contract runner, the Rust
evaluator and the registry's tests all read it. The touch tier replaced, in order, the 48px and
56px targets and 16px body of the registry before 1 November 2026, and the 44px touch, 24px
desktop and 15px desktop body that followed for two days; the desktop tier of 12 November 2026
is a 28px hit region and 15px body.

## How a control keeps its hit region

On touch, a control keeps a 49px hit region even where it is drawn smaller. The token layer's
`hit-area` rule gives every `button`, `summary`, `[role="button"]`, `[role="tab"]`,
`[role="switch"]` and any `.hit-area` element a transparent `::after` of
`max(100%, var(--target-floor))`. `--target-floor` is 49px on touch, and only the desktop
density layer lowers it, to 28px and not below. On a desktop a control is drawn 28 to 40px, so
its drawn box is its hit region and needs no pseudo-element.

The rule steps aside on purpose for an element that draws its own `after:` pseudo-element
(checkbox, switch, radio group, the tabs line variant), for menu and listbox rows (they take
`min-height: var(--target-floor)` instead), for a calendar day, and for an element with
`overflow: hidden`, which would clip it. Three consequences matter when you build:

- A native `input`, `select` or `textarea` cannot carry a pseudo-element, so the field roles
  (`h-field`, `h-field-sm`) are drawn at the hit region: 49px on touch, 36px (32px compact) on
  a desktop.
- A standalone link that is a target is drawn at the tier's size (49px on touch, 28px on a
  desktop), or takes the `hit-area` class. An inline link in running text is exempt, as in
  WCAG 2.5.8.
- Where two covered controls sit closer than 49px apart on touch their overlays overlap and the
  later sibling wins the overlap, so keep gaps from the spacing tokens.

## Three layers, two tiers

The layers are defined once in the token layer and emitted as media queries on the size
variables. A component never branches on screen size.

| Layer        | Applies when                                                                            | Controls drawn at           | Hit region | Body | Caption |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------- | ---- | ------- |
| Phone, touch | the defaults                                                                            | 49px (large 56px)           | 49px       | 17px | 14px    |
| Tablet       | `min-width: 48rem`                                                                      | 49px: an iPad is touched    | 49px       | 17px | 14px    |
| Desktop      | `min-width: 64rem` and `pointer: fine` and `hover: hover` and not `any-pointer: coarse` | 28 to 40px (fields 32–36px) | 28px       | 15px | 14px    |

On a desktop, large body text (`text-body-lg`) is 17px, a field is 36px (32px compact) and a
caption stays 14px. Only display and heading sizes step down as screens grow, and body text
steps down once, at the desktop tier. A touchscreen laptop reports a coarse pointer
(`any-pointer: coarse`), so the desktop layer does not apply to it, and neither does it apply
to a tablet.
Density is about visual size only: screen size is never used to guess network quality, and the
gaps between targets are spacing tokens that density does not change.

## Contrast beside the floor

The floor's contrast numbers are WCAG 2 minimums: **7:1** for body text, **4.5:1** for
non-body text and **3:1** for UI parts. The registry's token build fails when a default role
is under its minimum, and the APCA bars still apply. Five button-label roles hold 4.5:1 rather
than 7:1, an owner-confirmed exception: `primary-foreground`, `brand-accent-foreground`,
`app-accent-foreground`, `sidebar-primary-foreground` and `destructive-foreground`. The
thresholds, the reason for the exception and how to test a pairing are on
[accessibility](/foundations/accessibility#contrast-wcag-minimums-and-apca).

## Migrating from the earlier sizes

"Earlier" is the registry's 1 November 2026 sizes (4.21.0 to 4.22.x). Nothing changes for an
app until it takes the new registry version or `@bundu/ui` 0.8.0.

| Token                                    | Earlier (1 November 2026) | Now: phone, tablet | Now: desktop    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------ | --------------- |
| `--size-touch`, `--size-control`         | 44px; 36px on desktop     | 49px               | 36px (hit 36px) |
| `--size-control-sm`                      | 44px; 32px on desktop     | 49px               | 32px (hit 32px) |
| `--size-control-lg`                      | 48px; 40px on desktop     | 56px               | 40px (hit 40px) |
| `--size-field`, `--size-field-sm`        | 44px; 36px, 32px          | 49px               | 36px, 32px      |
| `--target-floor`                         | 44px; 24px on desktop     | 49px               | 28px            |
| `--fs-body`                              | 17px; 16px; 15px          | 17px               | 15px            |
| `--fs-small` (`text-body-sm`, `text-sm`) | 15px; 14px                | 14px               | 14px            |
| `--fs-caption` (`text-xs`)               | 12px                      | 14px               | 14px            |
| Badge text, chart axis labels            | 10 to 12px                | 14px               | 14px            |

In an app:

1. Take the registry version (or `@bundu/ui` built from it) and re-run `pnpm tokens:sync` or
   re-copy `mzizi-tokens-globals.css`.
2. Replace `h-12`, `h-14` and `min-h-[44px]` on a control with a size role (`h-control`,
   `h-field`, `min-h-touch-min`). A drawn size under 49px is fine on a desktop, down to 28px,
   and never on touch.
3. Replace any `text-[Npx]` under 14 with `text-sm`, and write body copy as `text-body`.
4. If a layout breaks and cannot move yet, set the legacy alias (below) and plan the move.

Expect denser layouts to need room: a badge or a meta label that fitted at 10px is now 14px.

### The legacy alias

To take the version and keep the earlier sizes for a while, put the attribute on the root
element:

```html
<html data-mzizi-sizes="legacy">
```

It restores the sizes from before 1 November 2026 (a 48px floor, 56px and 48px controls, 16px
body, 12px captions) and outranks the density layer; it does not restore the 1 November
sizes in the table above. It **steps out of the floor**: that is the point of it, and the
reason to remove it as soon as the app has moved. It is a compatibility alias, and the
registry plans to remove it in the next major version. `text-xs` and `text-body` follow the
same variables, so they step out with it.

### One class name to avoid

Write body copy as `text-body`, not `text-base`. The token layer registers `--text-base`
(17px), but a theme that also defines a colour named `base` (`--color-base`) can make
`text-base` ambiguous. The registry has not yet decided how to settle it; `text-body`,
`text-sm` and `text-xs` work meanwhile.

## What fails the build

The registry's `bundu-floor` test fails when a default type step is under its floor in any
layer, when a floored size role is under its tier's minimum (49px on touch), when the desktop
layer lowers `--target-floor` below 28px or the `hit-area` rule is gone, when a desktop control
is drawn under 28px, when a copy of the floor disagrees with `BUNDU_FLOOR`, or when a registry
component names an arbitrary text size under 14px. The contract runner and the Rust evaluator
fail a density row whose interactive control has a hit region under 49px on a coarse pointer or
28px on a fine one. Rows that predate the floor are pinned, so a new one cannot join them
unseen; see
[the Dashboard Standard's density table](/patterns/dashboard-standard#density).

## The Bundu Order

The Bundu Order draws on biblical mathematical patterns and prime numbers (owner, 2
November 2026). This section states only what the registry's code holds, in its
`brand-guidelines` doctrine page.

- **The unit is 7px** (`--radius-unit`). The radii are 4, 7, 12, 14 and 17px and a pill, and
  they do not change.
- **P7 and P17** are prime steps of the surface-elevation ladder (`surface` is P7, `raised` is
  P17), and the seven experimental colour families are a heptagon of hues offset 17 degrees.
- **The floors derive from the same numbers.** Body text is 17px on touch, a prime and Apple's
  iOS default, and 15px on a desktop (the owner's number). Captions are 14px (2 × 7). Every
  control keeps a 49px hit region on touch (7 × 7, above Apple's 44 pt) and a 28px one on a
  desktop (4 × 7, Apple's macOS default control), never with a smaller hit region.

The sources are the registry's
[`sizes-and-density.md`](https://github.com/mzizi-dev/mzizi-registry/blob/main/docs/sizes-and-density.md)
(the migration guide) and its `content/doctrine/documentation/brand-guidelines.mdx`.
