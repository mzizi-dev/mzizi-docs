The Bundu floor sets numbers that the other systems recommend, and sometimes do not set at all.
This page puts the floor beside WCAG 2.2, Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, Google's Material 3
and Anthropic's brand guidelines. Mzizi enforces its numbers. The other systems recommend most of
theirs, and an app or a review can still ship under them.

<Note>
**The floor shown here is the owner's ruling of 19 November 2026.** See
[the Bundu floor](/foundations/bundu-floor) for the rule and its history.
</Note>

## The numbers

**Enforced** means the system holds the value in its own checks. **Recommended** means it
advises the value. **Not set** means the system gives no value.

<Tip>
Mzizi's touch floor is 1px above WCAG AAA and Apple's 44, and Material asks for 3px more than
Mzizi. The Bundu floor uses a 3, 7 and prime rhythm, where Material and most tools count in 4 and
8. Designers coming from Figma kits will notice the difference.
</Tip>

## Contrast: Anthropic's palette against the floor

Anthropic's brand guidelines name colours and typefaces, and set no accessibility rules. The
figures below measure each accent against the brand's own Light (`#faf9f5`) and Dark (`#141413`)
backgrounds, using the WCAG 2 relative-luminance formula.

| Colour        | Hex                    | On Light (`#faf9f5`) | On Dark (`#141413`) | Reading                                                       |
| ------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Orange        | `#d97757`              | 2.96 : 1             | 5.90 : 1            | Decorative only on Light (below 3 : 1); non-body text on Dark |
| Blue          | `#6a9bcc`              | 2.78 : 1             | 6.30 : 1            | Decorative only on Light (below 3 : 1); non-body text on Dark |
| Green         | `#788c5d`              | 3.49 : 1             | 5.01 : 1            | UI shapes on Light (passes 3 : 1); non-body text on Dark      |
| Mid Gray      | `#b0aea5`              | 2.11 : 1             | 8.29 : 1            | Passes 7 : 1 on Dark only                                     |
| Dark on Light | `#141413` on `#faf9f5` | 17.50 : 1            | —                   | Body text passes the 7 : 1 floor                              |

On Light, Green passes 3 : 1 for UI shapes. Orange and Blue fall below 3 : 1, so they are decorative only. None of the three works for text on Light. On Dark, Orange, Blue and Green pass
non-body text at 4.5 : 1 but not the 7 : 1 body floor. That fits how Anthropic uses the palette: the
accents are for shapes and highlights, and running text stays Dark on Light. The Bundu floor makes
the same split explicit and testable.

## What each system believes

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mzizi: Ubuntu, made testable" icon="ruler">
    - **Five pillars:** Family, Community, Society, Environment, Spirituality.
    - **Five principles:** Survival, Solidarity, Compassion, Respect, Dignity.
    - **Seven engineering constraints:** Outdoor readability, connectivity-gradient design,
      multilingual parity, shared-device awareness, personal data sovereignty, no surveillance
      business model, and the Bundu floor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apple: Human Interface Guidelines" icon="apple">
    The current principles are **Hierarchy**, **Harmony** and **Consistency**, which came with
    Liquid Glass in 2025. The earlier HIG named Clarity, Deference and Depth.

    - **Hierarchy.** Controls and interface elements elevate and distinguish the content beneath
      them.
    - **Harmony.** Align with the concentric design of the hardware and software.
    - **Consistency.** Adopt platform conventions that adapt across window sizes and displays.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Google: Material 3" icon="google">
    - **Personal.** Dynamic colour drawn from the user's own wallpaper and settings.
    - **Adaptive.** One system across phones, foldables, tablets, desktop and watch.
    - **Expressive.** Material 3 Expressive (2025) adds shape, motion and colour, grounded in
      Google's user research.

    Accessibility is built into components through state layers, touch targets and contrast roles.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Anthropic: brand" icon="palette">
    - **Colour.** Dark `#141413` and Light `#faf9f5`, with greys `#b0aea5` and `#e8e6dc`.
    - **Accents.** Orange `#d97757` first, then Blue `#6a9bcc` and Green `#788c5d`, used on shapes.
    - **Type.** Poppins for headings (Arial fallback), Lora for body text (Georgia fallback).

    These are brand guidelines for documents and decks. They do not cover interface accessibility.

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where the Bundu floor stands

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Ahead of the field" icon="flag">
    - 7 : 1 body contrast is WCAG AAA. Apple and Material ask for 4.5 : 1.
    - The floor is enforced, not advised. A build fails below it, where elsewhere a review might
      catch it.
    - Layered spacing floors: controls, cards, containers and page layout each have their own
      minimum. No other system here sets spacing minimums.
    - 1.5 leading by default. WCAG only requires that content survive it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In line" icon="circle-check">
    - 45px on touch is one pixel above WCAG 2.5.5 and Apple's 44 pt, and it is the number rule's
      value. 24px on a desktop meets WCAG 2.5.8.
    - Material's 48dp stays 3px larger. An Android-only product can still choose 48.
    - The previous 49px touch floor was 4px taller than 45px on every row of small phones.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watching" icon="eye">
    - **Text scaling.** Apple's Dynamic Type and Android font scaling grow text with the user's
      setting. Check that the px floors use rem or respect the OS text scale.
    - **A 3, 7 and prime rhythm.** Each Mzizi value is the nearest number at or above the
      recommended one: 9 for 8, 17 for 16, 21 for 16 on cards, 28 for 24. Designers coming from
      Figma kits will notice.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
